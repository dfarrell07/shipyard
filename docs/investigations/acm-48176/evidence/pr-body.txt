devel (#2324):

OpenShift's restricted-v2 SCC rejects UID 10000 on the netshoot and nginx-demo helper Deployments, leaving the service-discovery tests with no pods. Leave `runAsUser` unset on clusters serving `security.openshift.io` so SCC admission assigns an allowed UID. Keep 10000 elsewhere because older nettest images default to root.

The tests are in `test/unit/framework` because the unit runner skips `test/e2e`.

Tracks [ACM-48176](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/ACM-48176).
